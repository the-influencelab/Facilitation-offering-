Our Approach

We use a bespoke facilitation design framework, INTRO. Three stages, held together from the first conversation through to the final reflection in the room.

Design
Time spent with you and your co-hosts first, understanding the community in the room, the topics that matter to them, and what a good outcome actually looks like for the day. Nothing gets built until this is right.
Build
Personalised QR code interactive elements, live diagnostics, and in-the-moment breakouts, all built using simple, easy-to-use QR tech and interactive HTML tools. This takes the pressure off you: the tools do the heavy lifting, while the design stays genuinely co-created with you. We also offer videographers if you'd like the day captured.
Offer
Interactive, experiential sessions on the day itself, held for groups of any size, so every person leaves with a piece of insight, a moment of reflection, and something to act on.
